# Quarter-turn reorientation benchmark

In-hand whole-cube reorientation with the fixed-wrist Sharpa Wave, palm up, locked cube layers.
A goal is the current orientation turned by 90 degrees about a hand axis (x: palm normal, y: across
the palm, z: along the fingers). It is reached once the cube stays within 0.4 rad for 0.2 s; an
attempt fails on a drop, a layer turn, instability or after 6 s. Target: 90% goal success with under 5% drops. Generated by
`manip-rubiks-eval-rotate` (700 episodes, 71 s; chain axes `xyz`).

| Policy | Condition | Goal success (95% CI) | Command success (95% CI) | Goals / episode | Dropped | Timed out | Time to goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| xy60m_s0 | turn_+x | 100% (96%-100%) | 100% (96%-100%) | 1.00 | 0% | 0% | 0.9 s |
| xy60m_s0 | turn_-x | 100% (96%-100%) | 100% (96%-100%) | 1.00 | 0% | 0% | 1.7 s |
| xy60m_s0 | turn_+y | 100% (96%-100%) | 100% (96%-100%) | 1.00 | 0% | 0% | 1.6 s |
| xy60m_s0 | turn_-y | 100% (96%-100%) | 100% (96%-100%) | 1.00 | 0% | 0% | 1.2 s |
| xy60m_s0 | turn_+z_composed | 99% (98%-100%) | 98% (93%-99%) | 2.97 | 2% | 0% | 0.9 s |
| xy60m_s0 | turn_-z_composed | 100% (98%-100%) | 99% (95%-100%) | 2.99 | 1% | 0% | 0.9 s |
| xy60m_s0 | chain_composed | 95% (93%-96%) | 92% (89%-94%) | 7.12 | 4% | 34% | 1.2 s |

`turn_<c>` runs a single command per episode; `chain` runs random commands for 16 s, as in
training. Time to goal is the median time to reach the first goal. In the `*_composed` conditions
a z command is three x/y sub-goals (variant 0); goal success counts sub-goals and command
success counts commands, so it is the figure comparable with a direct z turn.
